Swing. Climb. Don’t get tagged.

A free VR tag game for Meta Quest. Use your arms to move, chase your friends, and stay out of reach.

Get the game Join the Discord

Try it: tap a player to tag them.

How it works

1

Put on your headset

Install the game on your Quest and jump into a room with friends or random players.

2

Push off to move

Use your hands to push off the ground and walls. Reach, swing, and climb to cover ground fast.

3

Tag or run

If you’re it, chase someone down. If you’re not, keep moving. Whoever is tagged takes over.

What’s inside

Free to play

No purchase needed to jump in and play with everyone.

Name tags for everyone

Every player has their name and ID above their head, so you always know who you’re playing with.

Made for Quest

Built for standalone Meta Quest headsets. No PC and no cables.
